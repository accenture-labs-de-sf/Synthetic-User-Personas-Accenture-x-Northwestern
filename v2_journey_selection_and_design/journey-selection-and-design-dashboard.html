What changed when we changed the mood

One person, Filomena Nguyen (30, operations manager in Parsons, WV), reviews a bank feature that helps cancel Netflix across 5 screens. We tested 5 ways of giving her a mood cycle. She remembers her earlier answers in every version.

The short version With no mood path (D0), Filomena writes long, polite checklists. Giving her a warm mood (D1, D2, D3) makes her shorter and friendlier, and she still likes the feature. Giving her a real recording that swings into anger and disgust (D4) changes both her tone and her final decision on Question 5: she says she would not trust the feature yet.
